What to Wear for a Cappadocia Photoshoot: Colours, Shoes and the Flying Dress

Published 2026-09-11

About a week before their trip, almost every couple sends us the same message: “What should we actually wear?” It matters more here than in most places you will ever be photographed. The backdrop in Cappadocia is not a green park or a city street — it is pale volcanic rock, soft beige valleys and, at sunrise, a sky full of colour. Some outfits glow against that. Others quietly disappear. Here is what years of dawns in Göreme have taught us.

Start with the colour of the valleys

The fairy chimneys and valley walls are cream, sand and dusty pink. Anything close to those tones — beige, khaki, camel, light grey — blends into the rock, and in the photographs you become part of the landscape instead of the reason for the picture. Strong, saturated colours do the opposite: they separate you from the background even from far away, which matters when we frame you small against a wide valley full of balloons.

Long and flowing beats short and tight

Movement is what makes photographs here feel alive. A long skirt or dress that catches the morning breeze gives us shapes we simply cannot create with a fitted outfit. Chiffon, satin and light cotton move; heavy denim and stiff synthetics do not. If you are choosing one outfit for the session, choose the one that moves when you turn around.

The flying dress: how it actually works

You do not need to buy or pack one. We bring our flying dresses to the session, and you can wear one over light clothing. The shot itself is a little piece of theatre: you stand still, one of us helps the long train into the wind, and the other takes the photograph at the moment the fabric is fully open. It usually takes a few attempts, and it depends on the wind — a gentle breeze is perfect, no breeze means more tries, and strong gusts mean we move to a more sheltered spot. If the flying dress matters to you, tell us when you book so we plan the locations around it.

Shoes: two pairs, not one

Valley paths are dusty, uneven and occasionally steep for a few metres. Arrive in comfortable trainers or flat sandals you can walk in without thinking. Bring the pair you want in the photographs in a bag — we will tell you when to change. Thin heels sink into soft ground and turn a relaxed walk into a balancing act. On smooth hotel terraces many of our guests simply go barefoot, as in the photograph above.

Dress for 5 a.m., not for noon

The sunrise session is the one people underdress for. Cappadocia sits around a thousand metres above sea level, and dawns are cold for most of the year — close to freezing in early spring and late autumn, below zero in winter, and still cool enough for a jacket on many summer mornings. Wear a warm layer over your outfit on the way; we keep it out of the frame and you take it off only for the shots. You will smile far more naturally when you are not shivering.

What he should wear

For men, simple and well-fitted works best: a linen or cotton shirt in white, black, navy, olive or a colour that complements her outfit, chinos or tailored trousers, and clean shoes you can walk in. Roll the sleeves up if it is warm. Avoid shorts, gym wear and anything with a big logo. Identical outfits tend to look staged — aim for colours that sit well together instead, such as her red with his black or cream.

Bring a second outfit

Outfit changes are unlimited during our sessions, and a second look is the easiest way to get two different sets of photographs from one morning — a flowing dress at the balloon viewpoint, for example, then something more relaxed for the cave hotel terrace or the lantern shop. Keep it simple: one bag, everything ready to put on in a minute.

Quick checklist

  • One saturated colour, not beige or khaki
  • Something long that moves in the wind
  • Trainers for walking, nicer shoes in a bag
  • A warm layer for sunrise, whatever the season
  • A second outfit if you want two looks
  • A hairstyle that can handle a breeze at the viewpoints

After you book we send a short style guide, and we are always happy to look at photos of the outfits you are considering and tell you honestly what will work. You can see what each session includes on our packages page.
